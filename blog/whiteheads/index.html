Key point

Whiteheads are closed comedones—small flesh-coloured or pale bumps caused by a blocked follicle whose surface opening has closed. They are different from pus-filled pimples and should not be repeatedly squeezed.

How do whiteheads form?

Oil and dead skin cells collect inside a follicle. When the opening stays covered by a thin layer of skin, the blockage appears as a small raised bump rather than a dark open comedone.

Whiteheads can occur on the forehead, cheeks, chin, jaw, chest and back. Clusters may make the skin feel rough even when there is little redness.

What may look like a whitehead?

Not every pale bump is acne. Milia, folliculitis, irritation, keratosis pilaris and some cosmetic-product reactions can resemble closed comedones.

  • Milia are tiny keratin cysts and need a different approach
  • Folliculitis often centres on hair follicles and may itch
  • Perioral dermatitis commonly clusters around the mouth or nose
  • Inflamed pustules contain visible pus and are not simple whiteheads

How are whiteheads treated?

Treatment aims to prevent follicles from blocking and to control any accompanying inflammation. Depending on age, skin sensitivity, pregnancy considerations and acne severity, a dermatologist may recommend topical medicines, selected procedures or changes to pore-blocking products.

Scrubs and frequent extraction alone do not address why new comedones form. Consistent treatment usually matters more than aggressive cleaning.

When is medical review useful?

Seek assessment when bumps are persistent, spreading, becoming inflamed, leaving marks or not responding to appropriate over-the-counter care. Review is also useful when the diagnosis is uncertain.

Frequently asked questions

Are whiteheads filled with pus?

No. A whitehead is a closed follicular blockage. A pustule is an inflamed acne lesion with visible pus and usually has surrounding redness.

Can moisturiser cause whiteheads?

Some heavy products may contribute to clogged pores, but moisturiser itself is not automatically harmful. Choose products labelled oil-free or non-comedogenic and suited to your skin.

How quickly do whiteheads clear?

The timeline varies with the number of lesions, treatment used and consistency. Acne treatment usually needs several weeks before its full response can be judged.
