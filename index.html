Popular Treatments at Aastha Skin Centre

Many concerns. One medically guided treatment plan.

The most suitable treatment depends on diagnosis, skin type, severity, medical history and expected downtime. A procedure is recommended only when it is appropriate for the patient’s concern.

Laser Hair Reduction

Laser hair reduction uses controlled light energy to reduce unwanted hair growth over a planned series of sessions. Treatment parameters are selected according to hair thickness, skin type and body area.

Acne Scar Treatment

Acne scars may require MNRF, microneedling, subcision, chemical reconstruction or fractional CO₂ laser in selected cases. Combination treatment is often considered because different scar types respond differently.

MNRF

Microneedling radiofrequency delivers controlled energy through fine needles to support collagen remodelling. It may be used for selected acne scars, enlarged pores and textural irregularities.

Q-Switched Laser

Q-switched laser treatment may be used for selected pigmentation concerns, tattoo removal and laser toning after dermatologist assessment.

Chemical Peels

Medical chemical peels use controlled solutions to exfoliate selected skin layers. Different peels may be recommended for acne, pigmentation, texture and photo-damage.

PRP and GFC Hair Therapy

PRP and GFC are supportive hair treatments that use growth-factor-based approaches. They may be included in a broader medical plan for selected types of hair thinning.

Botulinum Toxin Treatment

Botulinum toxin may help soften selected expression lines by temporarily relaxing targeted muscles. Facial anatomy and individual movement patterns are assessed before treatment.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers may be used to restore selected areas of volume or enhance facial proportions. Treatment planning focuses on anatomy, balance and natural-looking correction.

Acne and Acne-Scar Care

Active acne and scars require different treatment plans

Active acne treatment focuses on reducing inflammation, controlling blocked pores, preventing new breakouts and limiting the risk of permanent scarring.

Acne-scar treatment begins only after identifying the type and depth of scars. Rolling scars, boxcar scars and ice-pick scars do not respond equally to the same procedure.

When should I consult a dermatologist?

Consult a dermatologist when acne, itching, rashes, pigmentation, hair fall or infection persists, recurs or does not improve with basic care. Sudden skin changes, painful swelling, spreading redness and suspicious moles also require medical evaluation.
