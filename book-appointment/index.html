Consultation fee and follow-up policy

Consultation fee

The consultation fee is ₹500 at both Karan Nagar and Paloura.

Follow-up policy

One follow-up consultation within 10 days is included.

After the 10-day follow-up period, the regular consultation fee of ₹500 applies again.

The free follow-up applies to the same concern reviewed during the original consultation. A new or unrelated concern may require a new consultation.

Procedure charges

Procedure charges are separate from the consultation fee.

The final cost depends on:

Diagnosis

Procedure selected

Treatment area

Complexity

Number of sessions

Materials used

Aftercare requirements

Exact procedure pricing should be provided only after assessment.

Appointment and walk-in policy

Are walk-in patients accepted?

Yes. Walk-ins and appointments are both accepted.

Patients with confirmed appointments receive priority. Walk-in waiting time may vary according to:

Number of patients

Procedure schedule

Urgent medical cases

Doctor availability

Consultation complexity

Are same-day appointments available?

Yes, same-day appointments may be available depending on the branch and doctor’s schedule.

Patients should call or WhatsApp the clinic for the quickest confirmation.

Are urgent consultations available?

Urgent clinic consultations may be accommodated when the schedule permits.

However, emergencies such as difficulty breathing, facial or throat swelling, extensive blistering, high fever with rash or rapidly spreading painful redness should be taken to an appropriate hospital emergency department.

Preparing for your visit

What should you bring?

Bring the following where relevant:

Previous prescriptions

Laboratory reports

Current medicine list

Allergy information

Records of previous procedures

Photographs showing earlier stages of the condition

Existing OPD or patient number

Details of skincare or haircare products being used

Before a skin consultation

Where practical:

Avoid heavy makeup over the affected area

Do not apply a new medicated cream immediately before examination

Bring photographs if the rash changes during the day

Before a hair or scalp consultation

Where practical:

Avoid heavy oiling immediately before the visit

Bring recent blood-test reports

List current supplements and hair medicines

Note when the hair fall began

Frequently asked questions
