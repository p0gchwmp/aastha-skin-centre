Are PRP and GFC treatments provided in Paloura?

Yes. PRP and GFC hair treatments are available at the Paloura branch.

PRP, or platelet-rich plasma, uses a concentrated portion of the patient’s blood containing platelets and associated growth factors.

GFC, or Growth Factor Concentrate, is also prepared from the patient’s blood and provides a concentrated growth-factor preparation.

Their suitability depends on:

Type of hair loss

Duration of thinning

Condition of the follicles

Medical history

Age

Existing treatment

Realistic expectations

Several sessions may be advised, and results vary between patients.

Is skin biopsy available in Paloura?

Yes. Skin biopsy is listed as available at the Paloura branch.

A skin biopsy removes a small sample of tissue for laboratory examination. It may be recommended when:

A rash has unusual features

The diagnosis remains uncertain

A mole or growth is changing

An autoimmune or inflammatory condition is suspected

Skin cancer needs to be ruled out

The biopsy method depends on the lesion’s size, depth and location.

Is cryolipolysis available at Paloura?

Yes. Cryolipolysis and body-contouring treatment are listed as available.

Cryolipolysis is a non-surgical procedure that cools selected areas of localised fat.

It is:

Not a weight-loss treatment

Not a treatment for obesity

Not a substitute for diet and physical activity

Suitability depends on the treatment area, fat distribution, skin condition, medical history and expectations.
