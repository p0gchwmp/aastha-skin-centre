How do appointment requests work?

Appointments and walk-in patients are accepted at both locations. Patients with confirmed appointments receive priority.

Appointment requests can be made through:

Telephone

WhatsApp

Website appointment form

Email

Direct clinic visit

Same-day appointments may be available, depending on the branch and doctor’s schedule.

Submitting an online form requests an appointment. The appointment is confirmed only after the clinic contacts you.

What is the consultation fee?

The dermatologist consultation fee is ₹500 at both Karan Nagar and Paloura.

One follow-up consultation for the same concern within 10 days is included. After the 10-day follow-up period, the regular consultation fee applies again.

Procedure charges are separate and depend on:

Treatment required

Treatment area

Complexity

Number of sessions

Materials used

Aftercare requirements

Final procedure pricing should be discussed after clinical assessment.

What should existing patients mention?

Existing patients should provide:

Full name

Registered mobile number

OPD or patient number, where available

Previous consultation date

Clinic previously visited

Whether the request concerns follow-up, reports or a procedure

Providing the registered mobile number helps reception locate the existing patient record.

Patients should not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information through WhatsApp or the general contact form.

When should you not wait for a clinic response?

The clinic contact numbers are for appointments and clinic enquiries. They should not be presented as emergency numbers.

Seek urgent hospital or emergency care for symptoms such as:

Difficulty breathing

Swelling of the tongue, face or throat

Extensive blistering or skin peeling

High fever with a widespread rash

Rapidly spreading painful redness

Severe facial or eye swelling

Loss of consciousness

Serious bleeding

A severe medicine reaction

Do not wait for a website-form or WhatsApp response during a medical emergency.

Before visiting the clinic

What should you bring?

Bring relevant items such as:

Previous prescriptions

Laboratory or biopsy reports

Current medicine list

Allergy information

Details of previous laser or cosmetic procedures

Photographs showing earlier stages of the condition

Existing OPD number

Before a skin consultation

Avoid covering the affected area with heavy makeup where practical

Bring the names or photographs of products currently used

Do not start a new medicated cream immediately before the visit unless advised

Before a hair consultation

Avoid heavy scalp oiling immediately before examination

Bring recent blood-test reports

List supplements and hair medicines being used
