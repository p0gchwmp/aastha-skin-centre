Meet Dr. Cheena Langer

Dr. Cheena Langer is an MBBS and MD Dermatology qualified Consultant Dermatologist at Aastha Skin & Dermato-Cosmetic Centre in Jammu. She practises with a diagnosis-first approach because skin, hair and nail concerns that look similar may have different causes.

Her clinical work spans medical dermatology, laser dermatology, hair and scalp disorders, aesthetic dermatology and minor dermato-surgical care. Her wider professional work includes postgraduate research, scientific publications, academic presentations, invited faculty activity and conference leadership.

Care philosophy

Understand the concern before choosing the treatment

History, examination, previous treatment and relevant triggers are considered before medicines, investigations or procedures are recommended.

Approach to care

Diagnosis first. Treatment second.

A treatment plan should begin with understanding the condition rather than choosing a procedure first.

Understand

Symptoms, duration, previous treatment, medical history and relevant triggers are reviewed.

Diagnose

Clinical examination is used to identify the likely condition. Investigations may be advised when they can change diagnosis or treatment.

Explain

The diagnosis, expected course, alternatives, limitations and relevant aftercare are discussed in clear language.

Plan

Treatment is individualised according to the condition, medical history, skin or hair type, priorities and acceptable downtime.

Review

When follow-up is needed, response and tolerability are reassessed so the plan can be continued, adjusted or simplified.

Treatment principles

Dermatology without unnecessary treatment

No procedure without a clear indication

Not every skin or hair concern needs a laser, peel, injectable or other procedure. Treatment should match the diagnosis and the individual patient.

No identical plan for every patient

Similar-looking conditions may have different causes, and the same condition can vary in severity, triggers and response to treatment.

No unrealistic promises

Expected improvement, limitations, downtime, recurrence risk and maintenance are discussed where relevant. Results vary from person to person.
