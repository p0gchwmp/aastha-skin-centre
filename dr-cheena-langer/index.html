What was Dr. Cheena Langer’s postgraduate research?

Dr. Cheena Langer’s postgraduate thesis examined the morphological patterns of foot eczema and the identification of common contact allergens using patch testing.

Patch testing is a medical investigation used to identify substances that may be causing allergic contact dermatitis. This academic work reflects a foundation in diagnostic dermatology, eczema and contact-allergy assessment.

What is Dr. Cheena Langer’s approach to patient care?

Dr. Cheena Langer’s clinical approach begins with diagnosis rather than immediately recommending a procedure. Conditions such as acne, pigmentation, hair fall, itching and recurrent rashes may have several possible causes and therefore require individual assessment.

Her treatment approach includes:

Reviewing the patient’s symptoms and medical history

Examining the skin, hair, scalp or nails

Identifying possible triggers and underlying factors

Reviewing previous medicines and procedures

Explaining the diagnosis in understandable language

Discussing medical and procedural options

Setting realistic expectations

Planning follow-up and preventive care

When procedures are appropriate, treatment is selected according to clinically established indications and medical-grade protocols. No single medicine, laser or procedure is suitable for every patient.
