Qualifications and professional profile

Dr. Cheena Langer is an MBBS and MD-qualified consultant dermatologist in Jammu, with an MD in Dermatology, Leprology & Venereology and a Fellowship in Surgical & Cosmetic Dermatology from Sapienza University, Rome, Italy.

At Aastha Skin & Dermato-Cosmetic Centre, she evaluates skin, hair and nail concerns, including acne, melasma, pigmentation, hair loss and inflammatory skin conditions. Her professional work includes research, invited lectures and conference leadership.
