Comedonal acne

Blackheads and whiteheads are caused by blocked follicles. Treatment planning usually focuses on preventing new blockages and controlling accompanying inflammation.

A dermatologist can assess whether the pattern is mainly comedonal or mixed.

02 / Treatment route

A treatment plan should have an order.

The interaction keeps the page visually simple while retaining the medical hierarchy patients need.

01

Confirm that it is acne

Acne-like eruptions, folliculitis and other conditions can resemble acne. Examination matters before escalating treatment.

02

Assess severity and scar risk

The number and type of lesions, distribution, duration, pain, marks, scarring and previous response help shape treatment intensity.

03

Control active breakouts

Management may involve topical treatment, oral medicines, skincare adjustments or selected in-clinic procedures depending on the individual assessment.

04

Then address marks and scars

Once active acne is adequately controlled, pigmentation and scar-specific options can be considered according to the type of residual concern.

05

Maintain the improvement

Acne can recur. Maintenance, trigger management and follow-up may be part of the plan when appropriate.
