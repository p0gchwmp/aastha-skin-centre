Pigment-targeting laser · Jammu

Tattoo fading is a sequence, not one blast.

Laser tattoo removal uses short pulses of light to fragment selected tattoo pigment. Clearance happens gradually over multiple sessions and differs by colour, depth, density, skin tone and pre-existing scarring.

01 / Colour

Ink colour changes the wavelength conversation.

Different pigments absorb different wavelengths. Multicolour and cosmetic tattoos may be more complex than a simple black tattoo.

Black / dark blue

Dark pigments often respond more predictably to appropriate pigment-targeting laser treatment than many bright or pale colours.

02 / Course

Why the sessions are spaced.

01Assess tattoo + skin

Colour, density, depth, location, scarring and natural skin tone are reviewed.

02Deliver selected laser pulse

An appropriate wavelength and conservative settings are chosen for the target pigment and skin context.

03Heal

The skin needs time to recover; trying to clear everything in one session increases injury risk.

04Clear + reassess

The body gradually processes fragmented pigment and the next session is planned according to response.

03 / What changes difficulty

Small does not always mean easy.

Often more straightforward

  • Older tattoos
  • Dark ink
  • Less densely packed pigment
  • Little pre-existing scarring

Often more difficult

  • Layered professional or cover-up tattoos
  • Multicolour or cosmetic pigment
  • White/beige/flesh-tone ink
  • Existing scar or inflammatory reaction
✦
The goal can be removal, major fading or cover-up preparation.

Complete clearance is not guaranteed. Some tattoos leave residual colour, uneven clearance, pre-existing scar visibility or a faint ghost outline.
