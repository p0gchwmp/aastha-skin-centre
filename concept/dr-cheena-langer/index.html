03 / Consultation

What happens when you come in?

A scrollable treatment journey replaces a cluster of identical cards.

Drag / swipe through the consultation

01 / Understand

Tell us what has been happening.

Symptoms, duration, previous treatment, medical history and relevant triggers are reviewed.

02 / Examine

Look for the pattern.

Clinical examination helps establish the likely diagnosis and whether investigations may change management.

03 / Explain

Make the diagnosis understandable.

Options, limitations, expected course and relevant aftercare are discussed in clear language.

04 / Plan

Choose what actually fits.

The plan considers diagnosis, skin or hair type, medical history, priorities, tolerability and acceptable downtime.

05 / Review

Reassess the response.

When follow-up is needed, progress and tolerability are reviewed so the plan can be continued, adjusted or simplified.
