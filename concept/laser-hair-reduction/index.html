Laser hair reduction targets pigment in suitable hair follicles. Response depends on hair colour, thickness, skin tone, treatment area, hair cycle, laser parameters and hormonal influences.

05 / Safety & expectations

Good laser work is mostly about judgement.

Can laser hair reduction be done on darker skin?
Yes, many darker skin tones can be treated when the device, wavelength, settings and cooling are selected carefully. Poorly chosen settings can increase the risk of burns or pigment changes.
Why call it reduction instead of permanent removal?
Because not every follicle responds equally, hair grows in cycles, hormonal factors can activate new growth, and maintenance may be needed. The realistic endpoint is long-term reduction in regrowing hair, not a guarantee that every hair disappears forever.
Does facial hair need different thinking?
Often yes. Facial hair can be influenced by hormones, especially when there are symptoms such as irregular cycles, acne or scalp thinning. That context can affect expectations and maintenance.
