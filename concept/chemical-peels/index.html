Medical resurfacing · Jammu

Chemical peels, selected by skin + diagnosis.

Chemical peels are controlled skin-resurfacing procedures. The solution, strength and schedule should follow the concern, skin tone, sensitivity and previous treatment—not a generic “stronger is better” ladder.

01 / Peel depth

Depth changes both potential benefit and recovery.

Chemical peels range from superficial to deeper treatments, and the depth affects downtime, risk and suitability.

Superficial peels

Often considered for selected acne, blackheads, post-acne marks, mild pigmentation, dullness and superficial roughness, with comparatively less recovery.

03 / Concern matching

Peels can support a plan. They are rarely the whole plan.

01Acne

Selected superficial peels may support pore clearance and post-acne pigmentation, but moderate or severe acne can still require prescription treatment.

02Pigmentation

Post-inflammatory pigmentation, uneven tone and selected melasma cases may use peels alongside photoprotection and medical treatment.

03Acne scars

Peels may help colour and superficial texture but do not reliably lift deep rolling, boxcar or tethered scars by themselves.

04Maintenance

Sun protection, gentle skincare and control of active inflammation remain important between procedures.

04 / Safety

Medical-grade activity means medical-grade caution.

  • Assess diagnosis and skin tone first
  • Review active acne, eczema or irritation
  • Check previous peels, burns or laser reactions
  • Review pregnancy, medicines and current skincare
  • Plan sun protection before and after treatment
  • Choose peel depth and agent deliberately
  • Avoid treating already irritated or damaged skin
  • Set realistic expectations for pigment and scars

Not a salon facial.

Chemical peel solutions can cause burns, pigmentation change or scarring when used incorrectly. Strong peel products should not be treated as casual home exfoliation.
