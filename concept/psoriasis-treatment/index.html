Chronic inflammatory dermatology · Jammu

Psoriasis is a disease pattern, not dry skin.

Psoriasis is a long-lasting immune-mediated inflammatory disease that can affect skin, scalp, nails, folds and sometimes joints. Care is built around extent, body area, symptoms, triggers and long-term control.

01 / Patterns

Psoriasis can look very different depending on where it appears.

Plaque psoriasisRaised, thickened plaques commonly on scalp, elbows, knees or lower back.Scalp psoriasisScale, itching, burning and patches extending beyond the hairline.Inverse psoriasisSmoother inflamed patches in groin, underarms, breast folds or buttock crease.Guttate psoriasisMany small scaly spots, sometimes following infection.Nail psoriasisPitting, discolouration, thickening, crumbling or separation.Severe formsPustular or erythrodermic patterns can require urgent medical care.
02 / Skin tone

Psoriasis on Indian skin may not look classically “red.”

On medium-to-deep skin tones, active plaques can look violet, purple-brown, dark brown or greyish and may be mistaken for eczema, fungal infection or pigmentation.

During a flare

  • Raised or thickened patches
  • Dry scale
  • Itching, burning or soreness
  • Cracking or bleeding after scratching
  • Scalp flaking or nail changes

After inflammation settles

  • Temporary darker patches
  • Temporary lighter patches
  • Colour change can take months to fade
  • Colour change is not necessarily scarring
03 / Triggers

Look for personal flare patterns—not myths about hygiene.

  • Emotional stress
  • Skin injury or friction
  • Some infections
  • Changes in weather
  • Smoking and alcohol in some patients
  • Obesity and metabolic factors
  • Certain medicines
  • Stopping some medicines suddenly
  • Individual lifestyle or environmental triggers
  • Uncontrolled scalp or skin inflammation
04 / Care logic

Different body areas need different treatment strengths.

01Confirm psoriasis

Differentiate it from fungal infection, eczema, seborrhoeic dermatitis and other look-alikes.

02Map extent

Skin area, scalp, nails, palms, soles, folds and genital skin can change treatment selection.

03Screen beyond skin

Joint pain, swelling or stiffness should be mentioned because psoriasis can be associated with psoriatic arthritis.

04Build maintenance

Long-term control usually matters more than one short course aimed only at a visible flare.

✦
Psoriasis is not caused by poor hygiene.

Aggressive scrubbing does not remove the disease and can irritate skin. Face, genital skin and folds may also require gentler treatment than elbows or knees.
