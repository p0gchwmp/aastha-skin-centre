Hair-support procedure · Jammu

PRP & GFC, after the diagnosis.

PRP and GFC are blood-derived supportive procedures that may be considered for selected non-scarring hair-loss patterns after scalp and follicle assessment.

01 / PRP vs GFC

Similar idea. Different preparation.

Both begin with the patient’s blood, but the preparation method and terminology are not identical. Neither should be sold as a permanent cure for baldness.

PRP

Blood is processed to separate plasma containing a higher concentration of platelets. The prepared plasma is injected into selected scalp areas.

  • Autologous blood-derived treatment
  • Studied mainly in androgenetic alopecia
  • Protocols differ across clinics and studies

GFC

GFC commonly refers to a growth-factor concentrate produced from the patient’s blood. Exact processing varies by system.

  • Blood-derived supportive treatment
  • Early studies show possible benefit in selected patients
  • Evidence base is smaller and less standardised
02 / Suitability

Hair fall is not one diagnosis.

A growth-factor procedure makes sense only when the follicles and diagnosis are appropriate.

Gradual pattern thinning

Selected male- or female-pattern hair loss is the most common setting in which PRP or GFC may be considered as supportive treatment.

03 / Before procedure

The scalp gets assessed before the syringe appears.

  • Pattern and duration of hair loss
  • Hair density and calibre
  • Scalp inflammation, scaling or infection
  • Family history and rate of progression
  • Recent illness, stress, surgery or weight change
  • Pregnancy, childbirth or hormonal symptoms
  • Medicines and supplements
  • Previous hair treatments and procedures

Testing is selective.

Blood tests or scalp investigations may be considered when the history suggests anaemia, thyroid disease, nutritional deficiency, hormonal imbalance, infection or another medical cause. Not every patient needs testing.

04 / Session

A clinic procedure, usually with little interruption to routine.

01Review scalp

The planned treatment area and diagnosis are confirmed.

02Draw blood

A small blood sample is collected and processed in the selected system.

03Prepare scalp

The scalp is cleaned; anaesthesia or cooling may be used when appropriate.

04Inject + aftercare

The preparation is injected into planned areas and post-procedure instructions are given.

✦
Supportive treatment, not follicle resurrection.

PRP or GFC cannot reliably restore follicles that have been permanently destroyed by scarring and should not be marketed as guaranteed full-density regrowth.
