Ablative laser resurfacing · Jammu

Fractional CO₂, with controlled intensity.

Fractional CO₂ laser creates microscopic treatment columns with untreated skin between them. It may be considered for selected acne scars, texture and other resurfacing indications after skin-tone, scar and recovery assessment.

01 / Mechanism

Resurface in microscopic columns, not one continuous field.

Fractional treatment leaves untreated tissue between columns, which supports healing while treated areas undergo resurfacing and collagen remodelling.

01Assess

Scar depth, skin tone, pigmentation history, active acne and recovery tolerance shape suitability.

02Set parameters

Depth, density, energy and passes are selected for the indication and anatomical area.

03Create columns

Microscopic treatment zones are placed through the skin while untreated tissue remains between them.

04Heal & remodel

Redness, swelling, crusting or peeling may occur while remodelling continues over time.

02 / Scar matching

Laser is one tool inside a scar plan.

Rolling scars

Selected rolling scars may soften with resurfacing, but tethered scars can need subcision before or alongside laser.

03 / Suitability

Skin tone, inflammation and recovery all change the plan.

  • Confirm the primary concern and scar type
  • Check scar tethering and depth
  • Assess active acne or infection
  • Review skin tone and pigmentation history
  • Ask about keloid tendency
  • Review previous MNRF, peels and lasers
  • Check medicines and current skincare
  • Review cold-sore history
  • Discuss sun exposure and downtime
  • Compare laser with non-laser alternatives

No “maximum settings” mindset.

Stronger treatment does not automatically mean a better or safer result. Intensity must be balanced against recovery and complication risk.

04 / Compare

Fractional CO₂ vs MNRF.

Fractional CO₂

Ablative laser resurfacing that creates microscopic columns and can remove small amounts of tissue.

  • Laser-based resurfacing
  • Surface recovery can include crusting and peeling
  • Pigmentation risk requires careful planning

MNRF

Microneedles deliver radiofrequency energy at selected depths without using a CO₂ laser.

  • Needle + RF energy
  • Different depth and thermal profile
  • Often considered for selected atrophic scars and texture
✦
Active inflammatory acne is usually controlled first.

New breakouts can create new scars while older scars are being resurfaced. Laser should not be treated as a substitute for acne control.
