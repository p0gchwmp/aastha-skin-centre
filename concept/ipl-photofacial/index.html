IPL photofacial · Jammu

Broad-spectrum light, specific targets.

IPL uses filtered broad-spectrum light rather than a single laser wavelength. It may be considered for selected facial redness, visible superficial vessels, sun spots and uneven sun-related colour after diagnosis and skin-tone assessment.

01 / What IPL is

Light treatment is not one single thing.

IPL is not a laser. Filters and treatment settings select part of a broad light spectrum to target structures such as melanin in brown pigment or oxyhaemoglobin in superficial vessels.

Light source

Broad spectrum.

IPL produces a range of wavelengths and uses filters to shape the delivered light.

Vascular

Redness and vessels.

Selected superficial vessels and facial erythema may be considered for treatment.

Pigment

Selected sun spots.

Superficial melanin-containing lesions may respond when diagnosis and skin tone are suitable.

Not universal

IPL is not “for all pigmentation.”

Melasma, deeper pigment and suspicious brown lesions require a different level of caution and planning.

02 / Target explorer

What is the light trying to hit?

Selected target

Facial redness

Diffuse facial colour may improve when superficial vascular structures contribute to the appearance.

Redness still needs a diagnosis because rosacea, dermatitis and other conditions can overlap.

03 / Assessment

Diagnosis, skin tone and target decide suitability.

Target
Vascular redness, superficial pigment and textural concerns are not interchangeable treatment goals.
Skin tone
Melanin-rich skin requires careful selection because surrounding pigment can also absorb light.
Sun exposure
Recent tanning or significant sun exposure can affect treatment risk and timing.
Melasma
IPL is not a routine first-line option for every melasma patient and may worsen pigment in some cases.
04 / Treatment route

Define the target before choosing the device.

01 / Confirm

Know what is causing the colour.

Rosacea, contact dermatitis, acne, sun spots and melasma can require very different pathways.

02 / Prepare

Reduce avoidable risk.

Sun exposure, skincare, medicines and recent procedures may need review before treatment.

03 / Select

Filter and settings follow the target and skin.

The aim is controlled energy delivery, not simply turning the device “higher.”

04 / Treat

Expect staged improvement rather than an instant reset.

Several sessions may be needed depending on the indication and response.

05 / Review

Reassess colour, vessels and tolerance.

Further treatment, maintenance or a different pathway can be considered according to response.

05 / FAQ

Common questions.

Is IPL a laser?
No. IPL uses broad-spectrum filtered light, while a laser generally uses a more specific wavelength.
Can IPL treat rosacea?
It may help selected persistent redness or visible vessels as part of a wider rosacea plan; it does not permanently cure rosacea.
Can IPL treat acne scars?
It may help selected red or superficial brown post-acne marks, but it does not adequately correct deep textural acne scars.
