Eczema care built around the barrier + flare.

Eczema is an umbrella term for inflamed, itchy and excessively dry skin conditions. Atopic dermatitis is one form; contact dermatitis, hand eczema and other patterns may need different management.

01 / Eczema family

Not every itchy rash is the same eczema.

The original site distinguishes atopic dermatitis from contact dermatitis, hand eczema, dyshidrotic eczema, nummular eczema, seborrhoeic dermatitis and other patterns.

02 / Triggers

Find repeatable triggers, not an impossible avoidance list.

  • Dry weather and low humidity
  • Heat and sweating
  • Fragranced skincare
  • Harsh cleansers and detergents
  • Repeated handwashing
  • Wool or rough fabrics
  • Dust and smoke
  • Stress
  • Skin infection
  • Occupational chemicals
  • Contact allergy
  • Individual environmental exposures
03 / What it can look like

Eczema is not always bright red.

On medium-to-deep Indian skin tones, active eczema may appear brown, purple, grey, dusky or simply darker than the surrounding skin.

Active eczema

  • Intense itching
  • Dry or rough skin
  • Scaling or cracking
  • Oozing or crusting in some flares
  • Small bumps or thickened skin

After a flare

  • Temporary darker marks
  • Temporary lighter marks
  • Thickened skin after repeated scratching
  • Ongoing sensitivity even when redness settles
04 / Care logic

Repair the barrier. Calm inflammation. Prevent the next flare.

01Confirm the pattern

Differentiate eczema from fungal infection, psoriasis, contact allergy and other look-alikes.

02Reduce irritants

Review cleansers, skincare, workplace exposure, repeated washing and personal triggers.

03Treat inflammation

Prescription treatment is selected according to severity, site, age and skin condition.

04Maintain the barrier

Moisturising and trigger control help reduce repeated flare cycles.

✦
Eczema itself is not contagious.

An infection on top of eczema can occasionally be contagious, but the inflammatory skin condition itself does not spread through touch, towels or living together.
