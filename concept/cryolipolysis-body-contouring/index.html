Cryolipolysis · Jammu

Body contouring is about shape, not weight loss.

Cryolipolysis uses controlled cooling for selected localised, pinchable subcutaneous fat. It does not treat obesity, visceral fat or significant loose skin.

01 / Suitability explorer

The shape of the concern matters more than the treatment name.

Selected concern

Localised pinchable fat

A defined soft bulge beneath the skin is the type of tissue cryolipolysis is designed to target.

Applicator fit and anatomy still need assessment.
02 / Assessment

Separate fat, skin and structure before planning treatment.

Fat type
Whether the fullness is soft subcutaneous fat, visceral fullness or a mixture.
Skin quality
Laxity, scars, sensation and the quality of overlying skin affect suitability.
Medical context
Cold-sensitivity disorders, hernia, circulation concerns, previous surgery and medicines may matter.
Expectation
The goal is a limited contour change in a selected area, not liposuction-like transformation.
03 / Treatment route

Map the area. Fit the applicator. Let the result develop gradually.

01 / Map

Confirm the contour target and applicator fit.

The area is examined, photographed and marked only if suitable localised fat is present.

02 / Cool

Controlled cooling is delivered through the selected applicator.

Treatment time and settings depend on the device, applicator and anatomical area.

03 / Recover

The area can feel numb, tender or altered temporarily.

Immediate appearance is not the final result; the contour changes gradually as treated fat cells are cleared.

04 / Review

Assess contour change after enough time has passed.

Further treatment, if appropriate, is considered only after the response and anatomy are reassessed.

✦
Cryolipolysis is not a substitute for medical weight management, exercise or surgery when those are the actual needs.

The useful question is not “can this area be frozen?” but “what is creating this contour, and is cooling the right tool for it?”
