03 / Before you book

Common questions.

Is the appointment confirmed immediately?

No. The request goes to reception and the clinic confirms the final branch, date and time after contacting you.

Can I request a same-day appointment?

Yes, but availability depends on the clinic schedule and must be confirmed by reception.

Can I book a procedure directly?

Procedure suitability and preparation are usually confirmed after assessment. Contact reception before travelling for a scheduled procedure.

Are walk-ins accepted?

Yes. Confirmed appointments receive priority.
