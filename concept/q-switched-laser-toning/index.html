Pigment-targeting laser · Jammu

Treat the pigment diagnosis, not just the colour.

Q-switched lasers deliver very short, high-energy pulses and may be considered for selected pigmentation concerns. Laser toning uses a different low-fluence strategy across a wider area and should not be confused with treating one isolated brown spot.

01 / Two approaches

Spot treatment and laser toning are not the same thing.

Defined pigment spot

A selected isolated lesion may be treated with parameters intended for that specific pigment target.

  • Individual lesion
  • May darken or crust temporarily
  • Diagnosis must be confirmed first

Laser toning

Uses relatively low fluence across a wider pigmented area, commonly as repeated sessions.

  • Diffuse pigmentation
  • Gradual change
  • Recurrence and pigment complications remain possible

03 / Assessment

The laser setting comes after the diagnosis.

  • Identify the pigmentation pattern and depth
  • Review when it began and whether it is changing
  • Check for active acne or dermatitis
  • Assess natural skin tone and recent tanning
  • Review previous peels and lasers
  • Ask about medicines and hormonal factors
  • Review pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Examine suspicious or unusual lesions first
  • Discuss realistic benefits, recurrence and risks
  • Choose wavelength and parameters only when appropriate
✦
Laser toning is not a permanent cure for melasma.

Melasma is chronic and frequently recurs. Repeated laser treatment can also cause rebound pigmentation or mottled light patches in some patients, so long-term maintenance remains part of the plan.
