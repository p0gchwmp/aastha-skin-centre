Implementation note: This is a practical starter policy and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before the website goes live.

Information collected

The website may collect information a visitor submits voluntarily, such as name, mobile number, branch preference, preferred appointment time and a short appointment note.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, coordinate appointment requests, provide clinic updates requested by the patient and maintain operational records where applicable.

WhatsApp and external services

The appointment request form can prepare a WhatsApp message. Information sent through WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp’s privacy terms.

Sensitive medical information

Visitors should not submit detailed or highly sensitive medical information through a general website form. Clinical information should be discussed through an appropriate consultation or secure workflow.

Sharing and retention

The clinic does not intend to sell appointment information to advertisers. Information may be shared with authorised clinic staff, service providers required to operate the website, or authorities where legally required.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used, but no internet transmission or electronic-storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Questions
