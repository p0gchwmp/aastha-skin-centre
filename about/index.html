What is Aastha Skin & Dermato-Cosmetic Centre?

Aastha Skin & Dermato-Cosmetic Centre is a dermatologist-led skin clinic in Jammu providing medical dermatology, laser treatments, hair and scalp care, aesthetic dermatology and minor dermato-surgical procedures.

The clinic is led by Dr. Cheena Langer, MBBS, MD Dermatology, Consultant Dermatologist, who has more than 20 years in medicine. Patients can consult at two clinic locations in Karan Nagar and Top Paloura, Jammu.

Aastha Skin Centre is designed for patients seeking diagnosis-based care rather than one-size-fits-all procedures. Every treatment plan begins with understanding the concern, identifying the cause and explaining the available options clearly.

What is the clinic’s approach to dermatology?

The clinic’s approach is based on diagnosis, medical evidence and individual treatment planning. Skin, hair and nail concerns that appear similar may have different causes and therefore may require different medicines, tests or procedures.

The consultation process may include:

Review of symptoms and medical history

Examination of the skin, scalp, hair or nails

Assessment of previous medicines and procedures

Identification of possible triggers

Explanation of the diagnosis

Discussion of medical and procedural options

Guidance about expected results and limitations

Follow-up and preventive care

Procedures are recommended only when they are medically appropriate for the patient’s concern, skin type, health history and expectations.

What medical skin conditions are treated?

Medical dermatology focuses on diagnosing and treating diseases of the skin, scalp, hair and nails.

Aastha Skin Centre evaluates common, chronic and recurrent conditions including:

Acne

Eczema

Contact dermatitis

Psoriasis

Rosacea

Urticaria and hives

Vitiligo

Fungal infections

Bacterial skin infections

Viral warts

Molluscum contagiosum

Scabies

Skin allergies

Nail disorders

Paediatric skin concerns

Suspicious moles and skin growths

Patients are encouraged to seek medical evaluation when a rash repeatedly returns, spreads, becomes painful or fails to improve with over-the-counter products.

What acne and scar treatments are available?

Acne treatment at Aastha Skin Centre addresses active breakouts, blocked pores, inflammation, post-acne marks and the risk of permanent scars.

Treatment may include:

Prescription acne medicines

Medical-grade skincare

Chemical peels

Comedone management

Lifestyle and trigger guidance

Maintenance treatment

Acne scars are assessed according to their type and depth. Rolling, boxcar and ice-pick scars may require different procedures or combination plans.

Acne-scar procedures may include:

MNRF

Microneedling

Subcision

TCA CROSS for selected scars

Fractional CO₂ laser in selected cases

PRP-supported procedures

Combination scar revision

What hair and scalp services are available?

Hair fall may be caused by genetic, hormonal, nutritional, medical or scalp-related factors. A dermatologist-led assessment helps identify whether hair shedding is temporary, progressive or related to an underlying condition.

Hair and scalp services include:

Hair-fall evaluation

Male pattern hair-loss treatment

Female pattern hair-loss treatment

Alopecia areata care

Dandruff treatment

Scalp-disorder management

PRP hair therapy

GFC hair therapy

Hair microneedling

Scalp electroporation

Hair-transplant consultation and procedures

Hair-growth treatment usually requires time and follow-up because hair follicles grow in biological cycles.

What dermato-surgical procedures are performed?

Each procedure is preceded by clinical assessment, counselling, consent and aftercare guidance.

What can patients expect during their first visit?

A first consultation generally begins with a discussion of the patient’s concern, symptoms, medical history and previous treatment.

Patients may be asked about:

Duration of the problem

Allergies

Other medical conditions

Current medications

After examination, the diagnosis and treatment options are explained. Investigations may be advised when they are clinically necessary.

How does the clinic support patient safety?

Patient safety depends on appropriate diagnosis, suitable treatment selection, infection-control practices and clear aftercare.

The clinic’s patient-care principles include:

Medical assessment before procedures

Informed consent

Explanation of benefits and possible limitations

Sterile procedural practices

Appropriate patient selection

Procedure-specific aftercare

Follow-up where required

Avoidance of unsupported guarantees

Protection of patient privacy

Before-and-after photographs should only be used with written patient consent and without misleading editing.
